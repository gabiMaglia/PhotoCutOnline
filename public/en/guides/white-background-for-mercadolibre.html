White background for MercadoLibre: cover photo sizes and rules

Updated: July 2026 · Reading time: 5 minutes · By Gabriel Maglia

On MercadoLibre the first photo isn't "just another photo": it's the one that appears in search results, right next to your competitors'. And the platform is fairly clear about what it expects of that cover: product on a pure white background, centered, no logos or text on top. This guide explains the sizes, why a white background sells more, and how to get your photos ready free, without installing anything and without uploading them to any server.

Why the cover goes on a white background

It's not an aesthetic whim. The white background serves three concrete purposes on a marketplace:

  • Uniformity in the listing. When every cover shares a white background, the results grid looks tidy and your product doesn't "clash" — clashing, in a listing, reads as unprofessional.
  • The product is the star. With no distracting background, the eye goes straight to what you're selling. A busy background competes with your own product.
  • Consistency across listings. If you sell several items, the white background gives you a coherent visual identity effortlessly.

Recommended sizes

MercadoLibre works best with large square images. As a practical reference:

  • Format: square (1:1). It's the one that fits best in the grid and the product viewer.
  • Ideal size: around 1200×1200 px. At that size the zoom looks sharp and the buyer can see the detail.
  • Minimum: don't go below ~500 px per side; below that, the zoom looks blurry and reduces trust.
  • Weight: keep a reasonable weight with a good-quality JPG; you don't need a huge file if the resolution is already right.

Step by step

  1. Open the product photo in PhotoCut Studio, taken with the best light you have.
  2. Use the AI Cutout to separate the product from the background; touch up the fine edges with the brushes (handles, cables, transparencies).
  3. In Export, choose Color mode and set pure white (#ffffff).
  4. With the scale control, center the product and leave air around it: it shouldn't touch the image edges.
  5. Export square and large (for example 1200×1200) as JPG or PNG.

Your whole catalog at once

If you're uploading many products, doing them one by one is tedious. Batch mode lets you process several photos with the same cutout and white-background criterion, and download them together. It's the difference between preparing a catalog in an afternoon or in a week, and it also guarantees all your listings have exactly the same style.

Mistakes that scare off buyers

  • "Almost" white background. A dirty or grayish white shows next to a pure-white cover. Use #ffffff.
  • Product up against the edge. With no margin, the photo feels cramped. Leave air around it.
  • Shadows cut in half. If the original photo had a shadow, decide: either include it whole and tidy, or remove it with the cutout. A cut-off shadow looks like a mistake.
  • Logos or text on the cover. MercadoLibre penalizes covers with watermarks, prices or text on top. Save that for the secondary photos.
  • Uploading small. A 400 px cover looks pixelated on zoom and conveys distrust.

Frequently asked questions

Does the first MercadoLibre photo have to be on white?

Yes. MercadoLibre strongly recommends the cover show the product on a pure white background, with no logos, text or frames. The following photos can be in context or in use, but the cover defines how your listing looks in the results.

What size is best for MercadoLibre photos?

A large square image, around 1200×1200 px, so the zoom looks sharp. The recommended minimum is about 500 px per side, but the larger you upload, the better the detail shows.

Can I do my whole catalog's photos at once?

Yes. Batch mode lets you process several photos with the same cutout and background criterion, and download them together: useful when uploading a whole catalog in the same style.

Are my product photos uploaded to a server?

No. All the cutout and background change happen in your browser. The photos aren't uploaded to any server or stored once you close the tab.
