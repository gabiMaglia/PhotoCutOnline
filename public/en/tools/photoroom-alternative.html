Pick by use case, not by marketing

Photoroom is the better fit if:

  • You sell online and want templates and AI-generated backgrounds ready for a catalog.
  • You process high volume with a workflow built around the app.
  • You want advanced retouching, generative fill, automatic shadows and similar features.

PhotoCut is the better fit if:

  • You want to cut out or change a background and download it clean, with no account and no watermark.
  • You handle sensitive material — documents, client photos, NDA work — and don't want it uploaded anywhere.
  • You also need text, color palettes, icons or metadata stripping without installing anything.
  • You're on a modest machine, or want it to work offline once loaded.

Why ours can be free

It isn't generosity or a promo that will expire — it's architecture. Apps that process in the cloud pay for GPU servers per image, and that cost has to come back somehow: subscription, credits, watermark or caps. Here the AI model downloads once and runs on your processor. Our cost per cutout is zero, so there's nothing to ration.

The side effect is the one we care about most: with no server doing the processing, your photo is never uploaded. No cloud copy, no retention policy to read.

FAQ

Does PhotoCut add a watermark?

Never. The result is clean, at full resolution, with no export cap and no account.

Do I need an account?

No: open the editor and work. No signup, no expiring trial — there's nothing to manage server-side.

Can I use the results commercially?

Yes, with no restrictions from us. We never receive your photos, so there's no license to negotiate over them.

What does Photoroom do that PhotoCut doesn't?

Templates, AI-generated backgrounds, advanced retouching and ecommerce features at volume. We're narrower and more private.
