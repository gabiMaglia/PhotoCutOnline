How it works

The cutout combines two layers — the difference between a clean result and a "sticker" look:

  • AI Cutout: detects the subject and removes the background in a second. It handles about 90% of the work.
  • Brushes: you only fix the hard parts — hair, fingers, edges — with a keep brush and an erase brush, working zoomed in.

After the cutout

  • Transparent background (PNG/WebP): for logos, signatures, stickers, or reusing the subject in any design.
  • Solid color: white for documents and product shots, or any color you like.
  • Another image: with opacity control, to change the scene.

Private by design

Most background removers upload your photo to a server to process it. Here the AI runs on your own device, so the image never leaves your computer — ideal for client work, documents, or personal photos.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free and without a watermark?

Yes: free, no sign-up, and no watermark on the result, with no usage limit or hidden resolution cap.

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. The cutout runs in your browser; the image is never uploaded or stored after you close the tab.

Which format keeps transparency?

PNG or WebP. JPG can't hold a transparent background because it fills it with a color.

Does it work with hair and fine details?

The AI solves the silhouette and the brushes let you refine hair and edges while zoomed in.
