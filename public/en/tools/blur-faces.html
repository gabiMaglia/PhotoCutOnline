How to do it

  1. Open your photo in the editor (desktop or phone).
  2. Tap "Detect faces": the AI marks the faces in a second.
  3. Choose blur or pixelation and the strength.
  4. Tap a face to include or exclude it if you only want to hide some.
  5. Download the censored version and share that, not the original.

Why doing it without uploading matters

When you hide faces it's usually for privacy: minors, people who didn't consent, bystanders, or just background people. Uploading that same photo to a third party's server to "protect" it is contradictory — the file with the unhidden faces travels anyway. Here the detection model runs on your device and the image never leaves it. You generate the censored copy locally.

Common cases

  • Event or school photos with minors.
  • Screenshots or photos with bystanders who didn't consent.
  • Material for social media, articles or reports where identities must be protected.
  • Documenting something without exposing the people involved.

FAQ

Is my photo uploaded to detect the faces?

No. The model runs in your browser and the image is never uploaded.

Can the blur be reversed?

No, in the copy you download: the area is re-rendered hidden and the original face can't be recovered.

Blur or pixelate?

Both hide the identity. Blur looks more natural; pixelation has the classic censorship look.

Can I hide only some faces?

Yes: tap each face to include or exclude it.
