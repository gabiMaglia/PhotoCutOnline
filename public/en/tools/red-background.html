When red works

  • Promotions & sales: discounts, hot sales, launches. Red signals urgency.
  • Sports: energy, intensity, teams with red in their identity.
  • Social media: a piece on red stops the scroll better than almost any color.
  • Food: red stimulates appetite, which is why so many food brands use it.

The detail: don't let red "bleed"

Red is a dominant color and, especially with warm-toned subjects (skin, orange or red clothing), it can compete with the subject rather than highlight it. Two tricks:

  • Lower the intensity (a muted or coral red) if it oversaturates.
  • Leave a very thin neutral border around the subject to separate it from the intense background.

Which red to use

An intense red like #d12f2f maximizes impact; a modern coral like #e63946 feels friendly and current. For a brand, use its exact red (its HEX). Don't know the code? You can grab it from a reference image with the color palette & HEX tool.
