How it works

  1. Open your photo in the Filters sub-tool of the editor.
  2. Pick a filter (or leave it on "Original" and use just the adjustments).
  3. Move brightness, contrast and saturation until it looks right.
  4. Download the image with the filter applied.

The filters

  • Black & white: the timeless classic; raise the contrast for more drama.
  • Sepia: a warm, vintage tone.
  • Vivid: more saturation and contrast to make colors pop.
  • Soft: lowers the contrast for a gentler look.

Without uploading your photo

Unlike most online editors, here filters run on your own device. The image never travels anywhere — handy for personal photos or client work.

FAQ

How do I make a photo black and white?

Pick the "B&W" filter and download; raise the contrast if you want more punch.

Is my photo uploaded?

No: filters run in your browser and the image never leaves your machine.

Do I lose quality?

No perceptible loss: applied at full resolution and exported as PNG.
