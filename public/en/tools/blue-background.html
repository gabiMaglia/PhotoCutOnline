A blue background has two very different uses: the light blue many IDs and passport photos ask for, and the corporate blue that signals trust and professionalism on profiles and badges. Two blues, two uses

  • Light blue (≈ #c9e0f2): the classic ID-photo backdrop in many countries. Soft, uniform, doesn't compete with the face.
  • Medium / corporate blue (≈ #2f5fd0): conveys seriousness and trust; great for professional profiles and team pages.

Important if it's for a document

Light blue is common for identity photos, but each agency has its own rule: some require white, others blue, with exact head proportions, eye position and print quality. Many official processes also require the photo to be taken at an approved booth. Use this tool for non-official uses (profiles, internal badges, forms) or to prepare a base, and always verify the official requirement before submitting the photo for a document.

Step by step

  1. Open the photo in the editor.
  2. Use the AI Cutout and refine hair and edges with the brushes.
  3. In Export, pick Color mode and set the blue HEX (e.g. #c9e0f2 or #2f5fd0).
  4. Center the face with the scale control and export as JPG or PNG.

Frequently asked questions

Which blue is used for ID photos?

Usually a light, uniform blue (around #c9e0f2). Check your country's official requirement, as it varies.

Does it work for a passport or ID card?

Official documents often require an approved booth. This tool is ideal for non-official blue-background uses or to prepare a base.

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens in your browser; the photo is never uploaded or stored.
