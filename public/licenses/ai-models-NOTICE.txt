PhotoCut Studio — AI models used by "AI cutout" / «Recorte IA»
================================================================

The models run entirely in the browser (onnxruntime-web: WebGPU or
WebAssembly) and are served from this site. No image is sent to any server.
Devices with a suitable GPU use model 1; the rest, or any device where model 1
fails, use model 2.

1) BiRefNet-lite, 1024×1024, fp16 (main model on devices with WebGPU)
   Files served: /models/birefnet-lite-1024-fp16.onnx.part0
                 /models/birefnet-lite-1024-fp16.onnx.part1
                 (the two parts concatenated are the original file,
                 114,834,127 bytes, SHA-256
                 4059896039dfccb0f15b9080ff06d11d90e499449bb045e797055eb8901cf5f4,
                 checked in the browser before use)
   Model:        BiRefNet — "Bilateral Reference for High-Resolution
                 Dichotomous Image Segmentation", Peng Zheng, Dehong Gao,
                 Deng-Ping Fan, Li Liu, Jorma Laaksonen, Wanli Ouyang,
                 Nicu Sebe — CAAI Artificial Intelligence Research, 2024.
                 Original project: https://github.com/ZhengPeng7/BiRefNet (MIT)
   ONNX export:  https://huggingface.co/jiabins0303/birefnet-lite-1024-webgpu
                 (onnx/model_fp16.onnx), MIT. Its model card states that the
                 MIT grant covers the weights, which are unchanged; only the
                 graph topology was rewritten so it runs on WebGPU. It derives
                 from onnx-community/BiRefNet_lite-ONNX and from the
                 studioludens/birefnet-lite-512 export method.
   Changes:      the file is split in two parts to fit our repository; the
                 bytes are otherwise unchanged.
   Caveat (stated by the export's own model card): BiRefNet_lite lists the
                 DIS5K dataset among its training data, and DIS5K's terms
                 restrict commercial use of the dataset. The card notes that a
                 downstream MIT grant cannot resolve that question. We repeat
                 it here as stated; we make no further claim about it.
